Data controller

Florario – Flower calendar is a personal project. The controller of the data processed on this website is:

  • Owner: {{owner}}
  • Email: {{email}}

What data is processed

  1. Technical browsing data. Like any website, the server hosting Florario records technical data about each visit (IP address, browser, requested page, date and time) to serve the page and for security. We don’t use it to identify you or to build profiles.
  2. Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to know how many people visit each page, from which country, on what kind of device and from which website they arrive. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser: it recognizes a visit with an identifier calculated from the request and discarded after 24 hours, so it cannot identify you or follow you across days or websites. It may also record, without personal data, which buttons are pressed (for example, downloading a reminder or sharing on WhatsApp).
  3. Saved dates. If you mark dates with the heart (“Save”), the list is stored only in your browser using local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to any server and you can delete it by clearing the site data in your browser.
  4. Emails you send us. If you write to us, we will use your address and your message only to reply to you.

Florario does not use cookies, its own or third-party, for analytics or advertising, and has no sign-up forms. Cards and calendar files with your saved dates are created in your own browser: they are not sent to any server.

Third-party services

  • Hosting: the website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA), which processes technical browsing data as a data processor. Vercel participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Statistics: Vercel Web Analytics, from Vercel Inc., processes the visit data described above as a data processor.
  • Fonts: the fonts are hosted on the website itself, so nothing is requested from Google Fonts or other services.
  • External links: some buttons lead to TikTok, Google Calendar or the media outlets cited as sources. When you open them, those sites’ privacy policies apply.
  • Reminders (.ics): calendar files are downloaded to your device; we don’t know who downloads them and we collect no data when you do.

Legal basis and retention

Technical data and anonymous statistics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the website available and secure and in knowing which content is useful, and is kept for as long as the hosting provider determines for those purposes. Emails are processed with your consent when you write to us and are kept for as long as the conversation lasts and, at most, one year afterwards.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to {{email}}. If you feel we have not handled your request properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

If we add features that process new data, such as a newsletter, we will update this page before launching them. Last updated: {{updated}}.
