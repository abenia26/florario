Yellow flowers

Joy, friendship, hope and new beginnings. It is the easiest color to give: it works for partners, friends or family without sounding like a declaration.

  • Sep 21Yellow flowers
  • Mar 21Yellow flowers in Mexico
  • Mar 8Mimosa · Women’s Day (Italy)

Red flowers

Romantic love and passion: the red rose is the universal code for “I like you”. In December, in Mexico, red is also the color of the poinsettia and of the roses for Our Lady of Guadalupe.

  • Feb 14Valentine’s Day
  • Feb 7Rose Day (India)
  • Apr 23Sant Jordi
  • Jun 12Dia dos Namorados
  • 3rd Sat SepAmor y Amistad
  • Dec 8Poinsettia Day
  • Dec 12Our Lady of Guadalupe

Pink flowers

Affection, tenderness and gratitude. Less intense than red, which is why it is the color for mothers, friends and first gestures.

  • May · OctMother’s Day
  • Feb 13Galentine’s Day
  • May 14Rose Day (Korea)
  • Aug 1Girlfriend Day

Blue flowers

Loyalty, trust and calm. Blue flowers are scarce in nature, which makes them special; on social media they became “the flowers for guys”.

  • Oct 3Boyfriend Day
  • Nov 19Men’s Day
  • JunFather’s Day

Purple flowers

Admiration, respect and a touch of mystery, like the nameless admirer in “Un ramito de violetas”. They are given to your “purple person”.

  • Nov 9Purple flowers

Orange flowers

Warmth and energy. In Mexico it is above all the color of cempasúchil, the flower that, according to tradition, guides the souls to the altar.

  • Nov 2Day of the Dead

White flowers

Peace, purity and remembrance. Depending on the country, white means good luck, like the muguet given in France on May 1, or memory, like the chrysanthemums of All Saints’ Day.

  • May 1Muguet (France)
  • Nov 1All Saints’ Day
  • any timeSympathy

The same color doesn’t say the same thing everywhere

The meaning of a flower depends on the country and the moment. The white chrysanthemum is a cemetery flower in Spain, yet it is common in bouquets elsewhere. The mimosa of March 8 is a gift in Italy, whereas in Spain and Latin America 8M is above all a day of protest.

If in doubt, mix colors: a varied bouquet, like the tulips in the photo, almost never fails. And if no date feels right for you, remember that any day is a good day to buy yourself flowers.
