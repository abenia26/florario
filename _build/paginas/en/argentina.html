Dates for giving flowers in Argentina

These are all the dates in the Florario calendar that are celebrated in Argentina, with the exact day this year and next:

The birthplace of yellow flowers

The biggest trend in the calendar is Argentine. In Floricienta, the 2004 series starring Florencia Bertotti, the heroine dreams of someone giving her yellow flowers. In September 2021 TikTok revived the song to welcome spring, and since September 21 was already Spring Day and Students’ Day in Argentina, with picnics in the parks, the idea fit perfectly. You’ll find the whole story in the yellow flowers guide.

Other Argentine dates

  • February 14: Día de los Enamorados (Lovers’ Day), with red roses. More in Valentine’s Day.
  • Third Sunday of June: Father’s Day.
  • July 20: Friend’s Day, more about get-togethers than flowers, although a little yellow bunch is a nice gesture.
  • September 11: Teachers’ Day, in honor of Sarmiento.
  • Third Sunday of October: Mother’s Day, in spring, with roses and lilies.
  • November 19: Men’s Day, with blue flowers.

Flowers in season in Argentina

In Argentina the seasons are the reverse of Europe’s. Spring arrives in September with freesias, tulips, ranunculus and lilacs; October and November bring roses, lilies and peonies; summer, from December to February, is the season of hydrangeas (very Christmassy there), agapanthus and sunflowers; and autumn, from March to May, belongs to dahlias and chrysanthemums. At Christmas, the poinsettia is called estrella federal. See the seasonal flowers table.
