What the chrysanthemum means (depending on where you are)

Few flowers change meaning so much when they cross a border:

  • Spain, France, Italy and Belgium: remembrance of and tribute to the dead. It is the flower of All Saints’ Day and it’s best not to bring it as a gift to a dinner.
  • China: longevity and a peaceful life. It is one of the “four noble flowers” of traditional painting and is drunk as an infusion.
  • Japan: the emblem of the emperor. The Japanese throne is called the Chrysanthemum Throne and the 16-petal flower is the imperial seal.
  • Australia: the Mother’s Day flower, which there falls in May, in the middle of the southern autumn; in English they’re called “mums”, just like mothers.
  • Mexico and the US: a cheerful autumn flower, common in bouquets and on Day of the Dead altars alongside cempasúchil.

The chrysanthemum and November 1

Why the chrysanthemum, of all flowers? Pure logic: it opens in autumn, when the days get shorter; it is cheap to grow in large quantities; and it lasts for weeks outdoors in the November cold. During the 19th and 20th centuries the custom of decorating graves on November 1 came together with the flower available at that time, and the chrysanthemum became the flower of remembrance for good.

Around those days prices go up and the florists near cemeteries have their biggest sales of the year, so it pays to buy a couple of days early.

Types of chrysanthemum

  • Large-flowered or “disbud”: a single flower per stem, the cemetery classic.
  • Daisy or “santini”: several small buds per stem, ideal for mixed bouquets.
  • Spider: long, thin petals, like the one in the photo.
  • Pompon: round, compact little balls.

How to make them last

  1. Cut the stem at an angle, or snap it by hand if it’s woody; it absorbs water better.
  2. Remove all the leaves that would sit below the water: chrysanthemums have lots and they quickly spoil the water.
  3. Change the water every two or three days.
  4. At the cemetery, a potted plant lasts much longer than a cut bunch, and potted chrysanthemums are very affordable.
