Email

The quickest way to contact Florario is by email: {{email}}. We usually reply within a few days; in the weeks around big dates (February, May, September and November) it may take a little longer. You can write in English or Spanish.

What you can write to us about

  • A missing date: tell us what is celebrated, in which country or region, which flower is given and, if you can, a link to a news story or source.
  • A correction: tell us the page and which detail is wrong. A source is even better.
  • Flower shops and growers: if you’d like to share your experience of a date or a flower, or link to the calendar from your website, we’d be delighted. We don’t charge to be featured and we never publish sponsored content without saying so.
  • Gardening, wedding and lifestyle blogs: you can link to the printable calendar or embed the next date widget. If you want to publish the PDF or use it in your business, write to us first: it is free for personal use.
  • Press: for figures on flower dates and trends ahead of each date, write to us with your outlet and your deadline. You’ll find figures, images and the embeddable widget on the press page.

What we do with your data

We only use your email address to reply to you and we never share it with anyone. The details are in the privacy policy.
