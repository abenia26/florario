Dates for giving flowers in Peru

These are all the dates in the Florario calendar that are celebrated in Peru, with the exact day this year and next:

A country that loves trends

Along with Argentina, Chile and Uruguay, Peru was among the first countries where the yellow flowers trend of September 21 went viral, since there it coincides with spring. Since then almost every color date has been added: March 21, Girlfriend Day on August 1, the blue flowers of Boyfriend Day and Men’s Day, and the purple flowers of November 9.

The classic dates

  • February 14: Valentine’s Day, with red roses. More in Valentine’s Day.
  • Second Sunday of May: Mother’s Day, one of the biggest dates of the year for sales.
  • Third Sunday of June: Father’s Day.
  • July 6: Teachers’ Day.
  • November 1: All Saints’ Day, a public holiday, with visits to cemeteries; in many Andean areas families spend the day with their dead, with food and flowers.

The cantuta, flower of the Incas

Peru’s national flower is the cantuta (Cantua buxifolia), an Andean shrub with red, pink or yellow tubular flowers. The Incas considered it sacred and used it in ceremonies. Today it still grows in the valleys of the Andes and is a national symbol together with the cinchona tree.

Flowers in season in Peru

Peru has very different climates on the coast, in the highlands and in the jungle. In the Andean valleys cut flowers such as roses, carnations, alstroemerias and gladioli are grown almost all year; on the coast around Lima the seasons resemble those of the Southern Cone, with spring in September.
