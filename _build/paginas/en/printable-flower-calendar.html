Download the flower calendar

Choose the year and click the cover to download the PDF. Both have the same dates, but the movable dates, such as Mother’s Day or Amor y Amistad, are worked out for each year.

Prefer your phone to remind you? There is also the calendar in .ics format, which alerts you three days before each date, and the Google Calendar subscription.

What’s included

  1. A cover with the wheel of the year. The twelve months in a circle, each with its own color, and a flower on each date of the year when flowers are given. It is the same wheel as on the website’s home page, designed to hang on the wall.
  2. The {{n_events}} dates, month by month. For each month, the day, what is celebrated, which flower is given and where: from Valentine’s Day, Sant Jordi or each country’s Mother’s Day to the yellow, blue and purple flower trends.
  3. Seasonal flowers. A table of the flowers at their best each month in Spain, in Mexico and Central America and in Argentina, Chile and Uruguay, plus a quick guide to the meaning of each color.

How to print it

  • Size: portrait A4. In the Americas many printers use US Letter paper; if that’s your case, tick “Fit to page” so nothing gets cut off.
  • Scale: 100% or “Actual size” if you use A4. The margins are already included in the PDF.
  • Color or black and white: both work. In black and white the flowers are told apart by their outlines and the text reads just as well.
  • Paper: to hang it on the fridge or the wall, 120 to 160 gsm paper holds up better than standard 80 gsm.
  • Double-sided: to save paper, print pages 2 and 3 double-sided and keep the cover separate to hang up.

Ideas for using it

The calendar is designed so you never miss a date, but you can get more out of it:

  • At home: on the fridge or next to your desk, circling the dates that matter to you.
  • In your planner: the dates page fits in a folder or at the back of a planner, to check before each month.
  • As a gift: printed on good paper, with the first flower of the year already bought.
