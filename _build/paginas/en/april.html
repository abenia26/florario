Dates for giving flowers in April

April 23 is one of the biggest days for rose sales in all of Europe. In Barcelona and throughout Catalonia the streets fill with rose and book stalls: tradition calls for giving a red rose, usually with a sprig of wheat and a ribbon in the colors of the Catalan flag, and the book was added when the date came to coincide with World Book Day. You’ll find the legend and all the details in the Sant Jordi guide.

April has other dates with flowers, though not as the main gift:

  • Holy Week (in March or April, depending on the year): the processional floats are decorated with carnations, roses and other flowers.
  • Seville’s April Fair: a flower in the hair, almost always a carnation or a rose, completes the flamenca dress.
  • Girlfriend Day: in some countries it is celebrated on the first Sunday of April, although the date that circulates most on social media is August 1.
  • Children’s Day in Mexico (April 30): people give toys and sweets rather than flowers.

Flowers in season in April

In Spain April is spring at its purest: tulips, fragrant lilacs, ranunculus, freesias and stock, and at the end of the month the first garden roses and the first peony buds. In Mexico it is the season of purple jacarandas lining the streets; florists are full of calla lilies and lilies. In Argentina, Chile and Uruguay autumn moves on with chrysanthemums, roses and asters.

What to give in April

  • Sant Jordi: a red rose with a sprig of wheat and a book chosen with the other person in mind. Today it is given in every direction: partners, friends, family.
  • To celebrate spring: a bunch of lilacs or of tulips and ranunculus, at a good price.
  • For an April birthday: freesias, for their scent, or a bouquet in pastel tones. More ideas in birthday flowers.

Tips for the month

On April 23 there are roses everywhere in Catalonia, but their quality varies a lot. The charity stalls run by schools and associations are a good option; if you want a special rose, order it from a florist in advance. Put the rose in water as soon as you get home and cut the stem at an angle: well cared for, it lasts a week.

Lilacs don’t last long once cut; to make them last longer, crush or split the base of the woody stem a little and remove most of the leaves. And use April to get ready for May: Mother’s Day falls on the first Sunday in Spain and on the 10th in Mexico.
