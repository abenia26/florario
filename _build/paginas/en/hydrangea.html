What it means to give hydrangeas

The hydrangea is associated with gratitude and heartfelt emotions: it is a way of saying “thank you for understanding me”. In Japan, where it is called ajisai, it is the flower of the June rainy season and of temples that fill with blue. The color changes the nuance:

  • Blue: calm, trust and loyalty. It is the star flower of Boyfriend Day.
  • Pink: affection and gentle romance.
  • White: purity; very popular at weddings.
  • Purple: admiration, a good choice for purple flowers day.

The flower that changes color

The same hydrangea plant can produce blue or pink flowers depending on where it grows. In acidic soils the plant can absorb aluminum from the soil and its flowers come out blue; in alkaline soils the aluminum is locked up and they come out pink. That’s why blue ones are common in northern Spain, with its acidic soils, and pink ones in areas with limestone soil. Gardeners play with this by adding aluminum sulfate or lime. White hydrangeas don’t change.

When hydrangeas are given

Ever since Boyfriend Day became the answer to yellow flowers, the blue hydrangea has been the most sought-after flower in early October:

It also works as a thank-you gift, for Father’s Day or as a potted plant for someone who is moving house.

Hydrangea season

It is a summer flower. On October 3 it is at the end of its season in the northern hemisphere, and in the south it hasn’t started yet, so many of those sold that day come from greenhouses or from Colombia, which grows them all year:

In Argentina, Chile and Uruguay they bloom right in December and are part of the landscape of the southern Christmas.

How to care for cut hydrangeas

  1. Cut the stem at an angle and, if it’s woody, make a small 2 cm vertical cut at the base so it absorbs water better.
  2. Plenty of water: its scientific name, Hydrangea, means something like “water vessel”. Top up the vase every day.
  3. Mist the petals with water: hydrangeas also drink through their flowers.
  4. Away from heat: it is one of the first flowers to dry out with the heating on or in direct sun.

Revival trick: if it has wilted, submerge the whole head in a bowl of fresh water for 30 to 60 minutes. In most cases it firms up again.

Dried hydrangeas

At the end of summer, when the flowers start taking on green and ochre tones on the plant, they can be cut and left to dry in a vase with very little water. They last for months and are one of the prettiest autumn decorations.
