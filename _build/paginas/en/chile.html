Dates for giving flowers in Chile

These are all the dates in the Florario calendar that are celebrated in Chile, with the exact day this year and next:

Chilean dates

  • February 14: Día de los Enamorados (Lovers’ Day), with red roses. More in Valentine’s Day.
  • Second Sunday of May: Mother’s Day, in the middle of autumn.
  • Third Sunday of June: Father’s Day.
  • September 21: yellow flowers to welcome spring, right after the Fiestas Patrias on the 18th and 19th. Along with Argentina, Peru and Uruguay, Chile was one of the first countries to join the trend.
  • October 3: Boyfriend Day.
  • October 16: Teachers’ Day. More in Teachers’ Day.
  • November 1: All Saints’ Day, a public holiday, with visits to the cemetery.

Flowers with a Chilean accent

Chile’s national flower is the copihue, a red bell that hangs from climbing vines in the southern forests. In the middle of winter, between July and August, the aromo (mimosa) fills the hills and roadsides of central Chile with yellow. And southern Chile has specialized in peonies, harvested between November and January and exported to the northern hemisphere just when they are out of season there.

Flowers in season in Chile

As in Argentina, the seasons are the reverse of Europe’s: spring in September (freesias, tulips, lilacs), peonies and roses in November, hydrangeas and lavender in summer, chrysanthemums and dahlias in autumn, and camellias, calla lilies and aromo in winter.
