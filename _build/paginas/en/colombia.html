Dates for giving flowers in Colombia

These are all the dates in the Florario calendar that are celebrated in Colombia, with the exact day this year and next:

Amor y Amistad, Colombia’s Valentine’s Day

Colombia has celebrated lovers and friends on the third Saturday of September since the 1960s. It is one of the biggest retail dates of the year: roses for your partner, sunflowers and small gifts for friends, and the “secret friend” game in offices, schools and groups of friends. Because the dates are so close, in Colombia the yellow flowers trend of September 21 arrives almost at the same time. There’s more in the guide to Valentine’s Day and the other Valentine’s Days of the year.

Other Colombian dates

  • Second Sunday of May: Mother’s Day, the biggest date of the year for flower sales along with Amor y Amistad.
  • May 15: Teachers’ Day, for Saint John Baptist de La Salle.
  • Third Sunday of June: Father’s Day.
  • October 3 and November 19: blue flowers for Boyfriend Day and Men’s Day.
  • November 9 (and the October 9 variant): purple flowers.

A country of flowers

Colombia is one of the world’s largest flower exporters: many of the roses and carnations sold in the US on February 14 come from the greenhouses of the Bogotá savanna and eastern Antioquia. The altitude of the Andes, the steady light of the equatorial zone and the mild temperatures make it possible to grow flowers all year round. In August, Medellín celebrates it with the Feria de las Flores (Flower Festival) and its Silleteros Parade, farmers from Santa Elena who carry huge flower arrangements on their backs. More on the August page.

Flowers in season in Colombia

Because of its high-altitude equatorial climate, Colombia has no marked seasons: roses, carnations, chrysanthemums, alstroemerias, hydrangeas and gerberas are fresh and well priced all twelve months. It is one of the few places where almost any flower is always “in season”.
