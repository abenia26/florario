Dates for giving flowers in March

March has three dates in three weeks, and yellow is the color that connects them:

  • March 8: in Italy people give mimosa; in Russia and Eastern Europe, tulips. In Spain and Latin America it is above all a day of protest. All the context is in the Women’s Day guide.
  • March 19: Father’s Day in Spain, Portugal, Italy, Bolivia and Honduras, for Saint Joseph. Ideas in the Father’s Day guide.
  • March 21: the spring yellow flowers in Mexico and Central America.

In Valencia, the Fallas also have their floral moment: the Offering to Our Lady of the Forsaken, on the 17th and 18th, when thousands of falleras bring bouquets that are arranged to form a giant mantle. It isn’t a gift between people, but it is one of the biggest gatherings of flowers of the year in Spain.

Flowers in season in March

In Spain spring gets going in earnest: tulips, daffodils, hyacinths, freesias and ranunculus at their best, and the last mimosas and violets. In Mexico the jacarandas start blooming in the streets, and flower shops are full of sunflowers, calla lilies and daisies, perfect for the 21st. In Argentina, Chile and Uruguay autumn begins, with dahlias, asters and the first chrysanthemums.

What to give in March

  • 8M: a sprig of mimosa or tulips, as a recognition, and always alongside real support, never instead of it.
  • Father’s Day: sunflowers, a bouquet in blue and white tones or a plant that lasts: a small olive tree, a succulent or an orchid.
  • March 21: sunflowers, tulips or yellow roses.
  • To celebrate spring: a bouquet of tulips and daffodils, each in its own vase so they don’t harm each other.

Tips for the month

March is the best month to buy tulips in Spain: they are in full season and cheap. If you mix them with daffodils, first leave the daffodils on their own in water for a few hours, because the sap they release shortens the life of other flowers.

For March 21, just like in September, sunflowers sell out in Mexico: order them a few days ahead. And if you want to remember all these dates next year, download the calendar with reminders.
