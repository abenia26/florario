Girlfriend Day: flowers for August 1

Girlfriend Day is August 1 (in some countries, the first Sunday of April). It isn’t official, but in Mexico, Peru or Ecuador it works as a second Valentine’s Day.

Where Girlfriend Day comes from

Día de la Novia comes from the American National Girlfriend Day, celebrated every August 1. Like other dates born on social media, it jumped to Latin America through Instagram and TikTok, and in countries such as Mexico, Peru, Ecuador and Costa Rica it has become a kind of second Valentine’s Day in the middle of the year. In some places another date circulates, the first Sunday of April, but the August one is the most shared.

It isn’t official in any country and there’s no old tradition behind it, but that doesn’t make it any less worthwhile: it is the perfect excuse for a gesture towards your partner far from February 14, when everything is pricier and busier. Its counterpart is Boyfriend Day, on October 3.

Which flowers to give on Girlfriend Day

The rose is the queen of the day. The color lets you match the message to the stage of the relationship:

  • If you’ve just started dating: pink roses, tulips or a mixed bouquet. Affection, no rush.
  • If it’s already serious: red roses, the universal code for love.
  • If you know her well: her favorite flower, even if it isn’t “romantic”. Nothing says “I pay attention to you” more.
  • If she likes things that are different: sunflowers, summer dahlias or a wildflower bouquet.
  • If you live far apart: a home delivery with a note written by you (ask the florist to copy it word for word).

In August, in the northern hemisphere, sunflowers, dahlias and zinnias are in season and make very striking bouquets at a good price. Peonies, on the other hand, are out of season and will be expensive.

Ideas to go with the flowers

  • A handwritten letter. It’s what people keep the longest.
  • A plan: a picnic, dinner at home or recreating your first date.
  • A printed photo of the two of you tucked among the flowers.
  • A plant to look after together, if you like gifts that last.

How to make the bouquet last in the middle of summer

On August 1 it is hot in Mexico and the rest of the northern hemisphere, and roses suffer. Trim 2 or 3 cm off the stems at an angle when you get home, remove any leaves that would sit below the water, change the water every day and keep the vase away from the sun and from ripe fruit. That way the bouquet lasts a week.

If you like celebrating everything, also note down Valentine’s Day (February 14), Boyfriend Day (October 3) and your anniversary. With the “Remind me” button we’ll alert you three days before.

What to write on a Girlfriend Day card

Not sure what to write? Copy one, tweak it and write it by hand: it shows.

For your girlfriend

  • Happy Girlfriend Day to the person I want every day of the year with.
  • With you, even Mondays are nice.
  • Thank you for being my home, my favorite plan and my person.

If you just started dating

  • It hasn’t been long, but you’re already my favorite part of the day.
  • Our first Girlfriend Day. Here’s to many more.
  • I don’t know your favorite flowers yet, so I started with these.

If you’ve been together for years

  • Another year choosing you. And all the ones to come.
  • Many August 1sts have gone by and I still like you as much as on the first one.
  • Thank you for growing with me. I love you more than yesterday.

Funny

  • Happy Girlfriend Day. This counts for February 14 too, right?
  • I’m giving you flowers so you keep putting up with me.
  • I remembered all by myself. Well, with a little help from the Florario calendar.

Girlfriend Day flowers on any budget

Three options, from the simplest to the fullest:

  1. A single flower

    A red or pink rose, with a ribbon and a card. Ecuador and Colombia grow roses all year round, so they’re easy to find there.

  2. A small bouquet

    Sunflowers or gerberas, which are in full season in Mexico in August and brighten up any bouquet. They usually work out cheaper than a dozen roses.

  3. A big or mixed bouquet

    A flower box with roses and filler, or a mixed bouquet of roses, lisianthus and baby’s breath. Roses are what make it expensive: filler adds volume for less.

DIY: a flower box

Flower boxes are all over Instagram, and making one at home is easier than it looks.

Materials

  • A sturdy box with a lid (thick cardboard or a hatbox)
  • Plastic or a bag to line the inside
  • Wet floral foam (the green kind for fresh flowers)
  • Roses, carnations or whatever is in season, with short stems
  • Scissors and a card

Step by step

  1. Line the inside of the box with plastic so the cardboard doesn’t get wet.
  2. Place the foam in a bucket of water and let it sink on its own, without pushing it down: that way it soaks all the way through.
  3. Cut it to fit the box, sitting slightly below the rim.
  4. Cut the stems diagonally to about 5 cm (2 in) and strip off the leaves.
  5. Push the flowers in close together, starting from one corner, until the foam is hidden.
  6. Stick the card inside the lid with one of the messages above.
  7. Tell her to add a little water to the foam every two days so the flowers last longer.

Frequently asked questions

When is Girlfriend Day?

August 1 is the date most celebrated on social media and in Mexico, Peru, Ecuador and Costa Rica. In some countries it is celebrated on the first Sunday of April.

Where does Girlfriend Day come from?

From the American National Girlfriend Day, on August 1. Social media brought it to Latin America, where it works as a second Valentine’s Day.

Which flowers are given on Girlfriend Day?

Roses, especially pink or red ones. Also tulips, sunflowers, peonies or her favorite flower, which is always the safest bet.

Is Girlfriend Day official?

No. It isn’t a public holiday or official in any country, but florists and brands increasingly have it marked on their calendars.

When is Boyfriend Day?

On October 3. People give blue flowers or Hot Wheels bouquets.
