Published September 29, 2026

What Florario is

Florario (flor + calendario, “flower” + “calendar” in Spanish) is a free calendar with every date of the year on which it is customary to give flowers in Spain and Latin America: which flower is given, in which countries and why, with the seasonal flowers of each month. It brings together both the long-standing dates, such as Valentine’s Day, Sant Jordi or Mother’s Day, and the trends born on social media, such as the yellow flowers of September 21, the blue flowers of October 3 or the purple flowers of November 9. It is an independent project by Héctor Abenia: it doesn’t sell flowers or charge anyone to be featured.

Florario in figures

  • 26 dates for giving flowers: 17 classics, 7 social media trends and 2 remembrance dates.
  • 3 dates born from a song, such as “Flores amarillas” from Floricienta or Cecilia’s “Un ramito de violetas”.
  • 19 guides by date or occasion and 15 flower pages with their meaning and season.
  • 6 countries with their own page and seasonal flowers for three regions: Spain, Mexico and Central America, and the Southern Cone.

Stories ahead of each date

If you’re preparing a piece about a specific date, these guides cover the origin, the countries and which flowers are given, with sources at the bottom:

  • Yellow flowers (September 21) and their spring version, on March 21.
  • Blue flowers and Boyfriend Day (October 3).
  • Purple flowers (November 9).
  • All Saints’ Day and the Day of the Dead, the poinsettia and Our Lady of Guadalupe.
  • Mother’s Day in each country, which falls on four different dates.

Widget: the next date for giving flowers

If you run a flower shop, or a wedding or lifestyle blog, you can embed a box on your website showing the next date for giving flowers and how many days are left. It updates itself, uses no cookies, loads nothing from third parties and links to the full calendar. For now the widget is in Spanish. This is how it looks:

Copy this code and paste it wherever you want it to appear:

<iframe src="https://www.calendariodeflores.com/widget/" title="Next date for giving flowers · Florario" width="320" height="200" style="border:0;max-width:100%" loading="lazy"></iframe>

To show only one country’s dates, add ?pais= and the country code to the end of the address: ?pais=mx (Mexico), es (Spain), ar (Argentina), co (Colombia), cl (Chile) or pe (Peru).

Contact

For interviews, figures ahead of a date or any other question, write to abeniacamarahector@gmail.com with your outlet and your deadline. We try to reply before each big date. You can write in English or Spanish.
