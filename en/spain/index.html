Spain’s own traditions

  • Sant Jordi (April 23): in Catalonia, the biggest day of the year for rose sales. A red rose and a book are given.
  • Mother’s Day (first Sunday of May): unlike Mexico or most of the Americas, Spain celebrates it on the first Sunday, in the month Catholic tradition dedicates to the Virgin Mary.
  • Father’s Day (March 19): Saint Joseph’s Day. Sunflowers and plants are gaining ground over the tie.
  • All Saints’ Day (November 1): families visit cemeteries with chrysanthemums. That’s why in Spain chrysanthemums aren’t given as flowers for the home.
  • Teachers’ Day (November 27): Saint Joseph Calasanz.

There are also big festivals with flowers that aren’t about giving them to people: the Fallas Flower Offering in Valencia (March 17 and 18), the May Crosses and the Córdoba Patios Festival in May, or the flower in the hair at Seville’s April Fair.

The trends arriving from Latin America

Thanks to TikTok, the “color” dates born on the other side of the Atlantic are increasingly seen in Spain: yellow flowers on September 21, blue flowers for Boyfriend Day on October 3 and purple flowers on November 9, which in Spain is something of a homecoming: the song that inspires it, “Un ramito de violetas”, is by the Spanish singer-songwriter Cecilia.

Flowers in season in Spain

Spain has northern-hemisphere seasons: tulips and mimosa in winter, peonies and garden roses in May, sunflowers and lavender in summer, chrysanthemums and dahlias in autumn. You’ll find everything in the seasonal flowers table and the details for each month in flowers by month.

Frequently asked questions

When is Mother’s Day in Spain?

On the first Sunday of May. Carnations, roses and peonies, which are in full season, are the most popular flowers.

When is Father’s Day in Spain?

On March 19, the feast of Saint Joseph.

Are yellow flowers given on September 21 in Spain?

The trend was born in Latin America, but in Spain it is known through TikTok and more flower shops advertise it every year. It isn’t an established tradition.

Which flowers are taken to graves on All Saints’ Day in Spain?

Mostly chrysanthemums, but also carnations, roses and gladioli. On November 1 families clean and decorate the graves.

What is Spain’s typical flower?

There is no official national flower, but the carnation is the one most associated with Spain, with flamenco and with local festivals.
