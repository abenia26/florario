The big Mexican dates

  • February 14: in Mexico it is the Day of Love and Friendship, so flowers are given to partners and to friends too. More in Valentine’s Day.
  • May 10: Mother’s Day (Día de las Madres), a fixed date since 1922, when the newspaper Excélsior promoted the celebration. Serenade, family lunch and a bouquet.
  • May 15: Teachers’ Day.
  • November 1 and 2: the Day of the Dead, with cempasúchil to guide the souls to the altar.
  • December 8: National Poinsettia Day, the Mexican cuetlaxóchitl.
  • December 12: roses for Our Lady of Guadalupe.

The country of trends

Mexico has enthusiastically adopted every color date born on social media: the yellow flowers of September 21, to which it added its own spring version on March 21; Girlfriend Day on August 1; the blue flowers and Hot Wheels bouquets of October 3; the purple flowers of November 9 and flowers for Men’s Day on the 19th.

Mexican flowers that went around the world

Several flowers that are given all over the planet today are native to Mexico: the poinsettia (nochebuena), the dahlia (the national flower), cempasúchil and even the sunflower, domesticated in North America. Today the State of Mexico, with towns like Villa Guerrero, is one of the country’s great centers of cut flower production.

Flowers in season in Mexico

Central Mexico has a mild climate that allows flowers almost all year, with distinct seasons: calla lilies in winter and spring, sunflowers, gladioli and tuberoses in summer, dahlias at the end of summer, cempasúchil in October and November, poinsettias in December. Frequently asked questions

When are yellow flowers given in Mexico?

Twice: on March 21, with the arrival of spring, and on September 21, the original date of the trend.

When is Mother’s Day in Mexico?

Always on May 10, whatever day of the week it falls on. Roses and carnations are the most popular flowers.

Which flower is used for the Day of the Dead?

Cempasúchil (Mexican marigold), together with terciopelo (cockscomb) and baby’s breath.

When is Teachers’ Day in Mexico?

On May 15.

Where can you buy cheap flowers in Mexico City?

Flower markets, such as the Mercado de Jamaica, offer wholesale prices and a huge variety, especially in the weeks before the big dates.
