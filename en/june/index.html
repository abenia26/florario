Summer arrives in the north and winter in the south. June brings Father’s Day in almost all of the Americas, the Brazilian Valentine’s Day and the season of weddings and graduations.

Father’s Day on the third Sunday of June is celebrated in almost all of the Americas. Flowers weren’t the typical gift, but since the idea that “men deserve flowers too” caught on on social media, sunflowers and bouquets in blue tones sell more and more. You’ll find each country’s date and what to give in the Father’s Day guide.

On June 12, Brazil celebrates Dia dos Namorados, its Valentine’s Day, on the eve of Saint Anthony’s Day, the matchmaker saint. Red roses, just like on February 14.

Weddings, graduations and the end of the school year

In Spain and much of the northern hemisphere, June is the month of weddings and graduations. For a graduation, a small bouquet of sunflowers or yellow or orange roses conveys joy and admiration and is easy to carry during the ceremony. As a wedding guest, it’s not usual to bring flowers but rather a gift, but an arrangement sent to the couple’s home a few days later is a lovely touch.

The end of the school year is another occasion: many students say goodbye to their teacher with a flower or a plant, just like on Teachers’ Day. And on Saint John’s Eve, the 23rd, in some parts of Spain people gather herbs and flowers to make Saint John’s water.

In Colombia, Ecuador and the Andes, the high-altitude climate lets growers produce roses, carnations, chrysanthemums, alstroemerias and hydrangeas all year round: that is why they are two of the world’s largest flower exporters. This table is a guide: the season changes with the area and the year, and greenhouses extend almost all of them.

In Spain it is the month of hydrangeas, which start blooming in the gardens of the north, of lavender, of daisies and of the last peonies. The first summer sunflowers arrive. In Mexico there are plenty of sunflowers, gladioli and highly fragrant tuberoses. In Argentina, Chile and Uruguay it is winter: camellias, calla lilies and greenhouse flowers such as alstroemeria and carnations.

What to give in June

  • For your father: sunflowers, a bouquet of blue hydrangeas with eucalyptus or a plant for his home or office.
  • For a graduate: sunflowers or yellow roses.
  • For a couple celebrating: white and pink hydrangeas, very wedding-like.
  • For yourself: a bunch of fresh lavender, which you can then dry to scent your wardrobe.

Tips for the month

In the heat, cut flowers last less. Change the water every day, use fresh water and put the vase in the coolest part of the house. Hydrangeas drink a lot, and through their petals too: if they wilt, submerge the heads in fresh water for half an hour and they come back to life.

Frequently asked questions

Which flowers are given in June?

Flowers for Father’s Day (sunflowers, blue tones or plants), red roses on June 12 in Brazil and bouquets for weddings and graduations.

When is Father’s Day in June?

On the third Sunday of June in Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Ecuador, the US and other countries. In Guatemala and El Salvador it is June 17, and in Paraguay the second Sunday.

Which flowers are in season in June?

In Spain, hydrangeas, lavender, daisies, roses, the last peonies and the first sunflowers. In Mexico, sunflowers, gladioli and tuberoses. In the Southern Cone, camellias, calla lilies and alstroemerias.

Which flowers should you give at a graduation?

Sunflowers, for joy and admiration; yellow or orange roses; or a brightly colored mixed bouquet. A small bouquet that’s easy to carry is more practical than a big one.
