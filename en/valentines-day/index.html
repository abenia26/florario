Valentine’s Day flowers and the other Valentine’s Days of the year

February 14 is the biggest flower date of the year, but it isn’t the only day of love: Brazil, Colombia, Korea and TikTok each have their own.

Why red roses are given on Valentine’s Day

Valentine’s Day is the biggest flower date of the year, and the red rose is its universal code: it means romantic love and passion, an “I like you” understood in any language.

In Mexico and other Latin American countries February 14 is the Day of Love and Friendship, so giving flowers to friends counts too. For them, colors without so much romantic weight, such as yellow or pink, work better.

The week before: Rose Day and Galentine’s Day

  • 7Feb

    Rose Day · India

    It opens Valentine’s Week: each of the seven days before Valentine’s Day has its own theme, and the first one is for giving roses. It is everywhere in Bollywood edits and reels.

  • 13Feb

    Galentine’s Day · social media

    It was born in 2010 in the series Parks and Recreation: Leslie Knope celebrates her friends the day before Valentine’s Day. Social media turned it into a real plan with breakfast, shared bouquets and a group photo. Colorful tulips are the most common flower.

The other Valentine’s Days of the year

If you missed February, there are more couples’ dates spread across the calendar:

  • 14May

    Rose Day · South Korea

    In Korea the 14th of every month has a theme for couples. May’s is roses: if you follow K-dramas or K-pop, it will sound familiar.

  • 12Jun

    Dia dos Namorados · Brazil

    Brazil’s Valentine’s Day falls on the eve of Saint Anthony’s Day, the matchmaker saint. It became popular in 1949 with an advertising campaign. Red roses are given.

  • 1Aug

    Girlfriend Day · Mexico, Peru, Ecuador, Costa Rica

    It comes from the American National Girlfriend Day and on social media it works as a second Valentine’s Day. Roses are given. Everything is in the Girlfriend Day guide.

    In some countries it is celebrated on the first Sunday of April.

  • 3rdSat Sep

    Amor y Amistad · Colombia

    Colombia has celebrated its Valentine’s Day in September since the 1960s, for partners and friends, secret Santa included. In 2027 it falls on September 18. Roses and sunflowers.

  • 3Oct

    Boyfriend Day · Latin America

    The guys’ turn: blue flowers or a Hot Wheels bouquet. It’s all in the blue flowers guide.

Which flowers to give if you don’t want red roses

  • Pink roses: tenderness instead of passion; ideal for a relationship that is just starting.
  • Tulips: the Galentine’s flower, perfect for friends.
  • Sunflowers: friendship and joy, for the “friendship” side of February 14.
  • Peonies: very photogenic, although their main season is spring.

Frequently asked questions

Which flower is given on Valentine’s Day?

The red rose, the symbol of romantic love. If it’s for friends, on the Day of Love and Friendship yellow or pink flowers work better.

What is Galentine’s Day?

February 13, a day to celebrate your female friends. It was born in 2010 in the series Parks and Recreation and social media turned it into a real plan with shared bouquets.

When is Brazil’s Valentine’s Day?

On June 12, Dia dos Namorados, the eve of Saint Anthony’s Day.

When is Amor y Amistad in Colombia?

On the third Saturday of September. In 2027 it falls on September 18.

When is Girlfriend Day?

On August 1 in Mexico, Peru, Ecuador and Costa Rica, although in some countries it is celebrated on the first Sunday of April.
