Which flowers to give on Mother’s Day (and when it falls in your country)

There isn’t just one Mother’s Day: Spain celebrates it on the first Sunday of May, Mexico every May 10, half the Americas on the second Sunday and Argentina in October.

When Mother’s Day is in each country

The dates that fall on a Sunday change every year. These are the next two, worked out with the same rule the calendar uses:

Argentina3rd Sunday of OctoberOct 17Oct 15Roses and lilies

Other countries have their own date: Paraguay (May 15), Bolivia (May 27), Nicaragua (May 30), the Dominican Republic (last Sunday of May), Costa Rica (August 15) and Panama (December 8). The Florario calendar includes the four most widespread dates.

Which flowers to give on Mother’s Day

  • Carnations: the original Mother’s Day flower and the cheapest. They last many days in water.
  • Peonies: the classic in Spain; May is exactly their season.
  • Roses: in Mexico, the bouquet that goes with the serenade and the family lunch.
  • Lilies: in Argentina, where the date falls in full spring.

If you’re unsure about the color, pink is the one most associated with this day: affection and gratitude without the romantic weight of red.

Why the carnation is the Mother’s Day flower

Anna Jarvis promoted Mother’s Day in the United States in 1908 and chose the carnation, her mother’s favorite flower. From there the date, the second Sunday of May, and the flower spread to much of the Americas and Europe.

Why each country celebrates it on a different day

  • Spain: the first Sunday of May, the month Catholic tradition dedicates to the Virgin Mary.
  • Mexico: a fixed date since 1922, when the newspaper Excélsior promoted the celebration.
  • United States and much of the Americas: the second Sunday of May, because of Anna Jarvis.
  • Argentina: the third Sunday of October, so it coincides with the best flower season of the southern hemisphere.

What to write on a Mother’s Day card

Not sure what to write? Copy one, tweak it and write it by hand: it shows.

For Mom

  • Thank you for everything you do that nobody sees. Happy Mother’s Day.
  • You were my first home. I love you, Mom.
  • Everything good I have, I learned from you.

If you’re far away

  • I can’t give you a hug today, so I’m sending flowers. I’ll give you the hug in person soon.
  • Distance doesn’t count on Mother’s Day. Thinking of you, Mom.
  • May these flowers bring you all the hugs I owe you.

For grandma or your mother-in-law

  • Grandma, thank you for raising the person who raised me. Happy Mother’s Day.
  • To the best mother-in-law: thank you for making me feel part of the family.
  • For the mom of everyone in this family. Happy Mother’s Day.

Funny

  • Happy Mother’s Day. Yes, I cleaned my room (almost).
  • I got you flowers because a dinner cooked by me would be a punishment.
  • Mom, you were right. About almost everything. Happy Mother’s Day.

Mother’s Day flowers on any budget

Three options, from the simplest to the fullest. They work in any country: only what’s in season changes from date to date.

  1. A single flower

    A carnation: it’s the traditional Mother’s Day flower, it’s usually among the cheapest and it lasts a long time in the vase. A rose works too.

  2. A small bouquet

    Carnations with alstroemerias, which also last a long time, or a small bunch of daisies. Wrap it yourself or put it in a decorated jar, as explained below.

  3. A big or mixed bouquet

    Peonies (in season in Spain in May, but among the most expensive), roses and lilies. If you’d rather give something that lasts for months, a potted orchid.

DIY: flowers in a decorated jar

A recycled glass jar turns a simple bunch of flowers into a gift that comes with its own vase.

Materials

  • A clean glass jar (from jam or preserves)
  • Jute twine or ribbon
  • A piece of card stock for the tag
  • Small flowers: carnations, daisies or whatever is in season
  • Scissors

Step by step

  1. Soak the label off the jar with hot water and dry it well.
  2. Wrap the twine around the neck of the jar several times and tie it in a bow.
  3. Write one of the messages above on the card stock and hang it from the bow.
  4. Cut the stems diagonally so the flowers stand about one and a half times the height of the jar.
  5. Place the biggest flowers first and fill the gaps with the small ones.
  6. Fill the jar with fresh water and change it every two days so they last longer.

Frequently asked questions

When is Mother’s Day in Spain in 2027?

Sunday, May 2, 2027. In Spain it is always celebrated on the first Sunday of May; in 2028 it will be May 7.

When is Mother’s Day in Mexico?

Always on May 10, whatever day of the week it falls on. It has been a fixed date since 1922.

When is Mother’s Day in Colombia, Chile and Peru?

On the second Sunday of May: May 9, 2027 and May 14, 2028.

When is Mother’s Day in Argentina?

On the third Sunday of October: October 17, 2027 and October 15, 2028.

Which flower is given on Mother’s Day?

The carnation has been the traditional flower since 1908. Peonies are also very typical in Spain, roses in Mexico and lilies in Argentina.
