Each month has its own page with the calendar dates, the seasonal flowers in Spain, in Mexico and Central America and in Argentina, Chile and Uruguay, and a FAQ. If you’d rather see everything together, the home page has the seasonal flowers table and the full calendar, and there is also a printable calendar.

The seasons are reversed on each side of the equator: in March, while tulips are starting in Spain, Argentina and Chile get the dahlias and chrysanthemums of autumn. That is why each month splits its seasonal flowers by region. And in Colombia and Ecuador, with a mountain climate all year round, almost every cut flower can be found in any month.
