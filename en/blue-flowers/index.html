Blue flowers for Boyfriend Day

Blue flowers are given to guys on October 3, Boyfriend Day, and again on November 19, Men’s Day. It’s the answer to yellow flowers: this time it’s their turn.

Why October 3 is Boyfriend Day

Boyfriend Day was born in 2014 as #NationalBoyfriendDay, a US hashtag. When it reached Latin America it settled on October 3 and became the answer to the yellow flowers of September: if she gets a yellow bouquet, he gets a blue one.

It isn’t official in any country, but in Mexico, Peru, Chile and Colombia it is already one of the biggest trends of October, and it is starting to appear on social media in Spain.

Why blue flowers?

Blue is associated with loyalty, trust and calm. On top of that, blue flowers are scarce in nature, which makes them special: giving them says “you’re hard to find”.

Heads up: almost all the blue roses on sale are dyed. If you prefer natural flowers, see the list below.

November 19: International Men’s Day

International Men’s Day has been celebrated since 1999. On social media it caught on with the idea that “men deserve flowers too”: blue roses, sunflowers or Hot Wheels bouquets to break the habit of only giving flowers to women.

If you missed October 3, you get a second chance in November.

Which blue flowers to give

  • Hydrangeas: truly blue and very showy; also sold in pots.
  • Delphinium (larkspur) and iris: naturally blue, for a tall bouquet.
  • Blue roses: dyed, but they’re the ones that show up most in the videos.
  • Sunflowers: the alternative if you can’t find anything blue, especially for November 19.

Why blue flowers are so hard to find

It is estimated that fewer than 10% of flowering plants produce truly blue flowers: blue pigment is chemically difficult for a plant to make, and many “blue” flowers are actually violet. That’s why the blue roses in the videos are dyed, either by standing the stem in ink so it absorbs it or by painting the petals. It isn’t a trick, but it’s worth knowing: the ink can stain your hands and the vase water.

The natural blue flowers easiest to find in early October are hydrangea (the one that gives the most volume), delphinium, iris, agapanthus and blue thistle. Order several days ahead: in the week of October 3 florists in Mexico, Peru or Colombia sell out of blue. And if you can’t find anything, a white bouquet with a single blue touch, like a ribbon or a Hot Wheels car, does the job just as well. There are more ideas for guys in the Father’s Day guide.

What to write on the blue flowers card

Not sure what to write? Copy one, tweak it and write it by hand: it shows.

For your partner

  • You deserve flowers too. Happy Boyfriend Day.
  • Blue, like the trust you give me. Happy October 3.
  • Blue flowers are hard to find, just like you.

For a friend

  • For my best friend, because guys deserve flowers too.
  • Thanks for always being there, even if you never say it. Here, they’re blue.
  • Blue flowers for the friend who always shows up when it matters.

For family

  • Dad, this year the flowers are for you.
  • For my brother, who pretends he doesn’t like getting flowers.
  • A blue bouquet for the man who taught me never to give up.

Funny

  • Yes, they’re flowers. Yes, they’re for you. Yes, you can post them on your story.
  • I was going to get you a Hot Wheels car, but you deserve the whole bouquet.
  • Blue bouquet delivered. You can’t say nobody gives you flowers anymore.

Blue flowers on any budget

Blue is the hardest flower color to find, so it helps to know what to ask for. Three options, from the simplest to the fullest:

  1. A single flower

    A blue hydrangea. Each head is made of lots of tiny florets, so a single stem already looks like a small bouquet.

  2. A small bouquet

    A Hot Wheels bouquet you make yourself (the steps are below) or a few stems of statice or blue thistle, which are usually cheap and also dry without losing their color.

  3. A big or mixed bouquet

    Hydrangeas, delphinium or iris and some greenery. Dyed blue roses are among the most expensive and the first to sell out: mix one or two with white flowers and they look just as good.

Order several days ahead: in the week of October 3, florists run out of blue fast.

DIY: how to make a Hot Wheels bouquet

The other star of October 3 is the Hot Wheels bouquet: toy cars wrapped in paper and ribbons as if they were flowers, sometimes mixed with blue flowers. It’s cheap, it lasts forever and it has become almost as typical as the blue bouquet. Here’s how to make one:

Materials

  • 6 to 12 toy cars (Hot Wheels or another brand)
  • Wooden skewers, one per car
  • Hot glue, or double-sided tape if he wants to take them off to play with or collect
  • A base: a block of dry floral foam, styrofoam or a paper cup filled with scrunched-up paper
  • Korean wrapping paper or tissue paper (blue, black or white), ribbon and a card
  • Optional: blue flowers, candy or chocolates to fill the gaps

Step by step

  1. Glue each car to the tip of a skewer by its underside, without covering the wheels.
  2. Cut the skewers to different heights: longer in the middle and shorter around the edge, so the bouquet has a dome shape.
  3. Push the skewers into the base, starting in the center and angling them slightly outward.
  4. Fill the gaps with blue flowers, scrunched-up paper or candy.
  5. Wrap the base in two or three sheets of paper in a cone shape, alternating colors so they all show.
  6. Tie the bouquet with ribbon just below the cars.
  7. Hang the card with one of the messages above.

Frequently asked questions

When is Boyfriend Day?

On October 3. It was born in 2014 as #NationalBoyfriendDay and in Latin America it is celebrated by giving blue flowers.

Why are blue flowers given on October 3?

Because Boyfriend Day became the answer to the yellow flowers of September 21. Blue is associated with loyalty and trust, and blue flowers are uncommon, which makes them special.

What is a Hot Wheels bouquet?

A bouquet made with Hot Wheels toy cars wrapped in paper and ribbons, sometimes with blue flowers. It is one of the typical Boyfriend Day gifts.

When are blue flowers given?

On October 3, Boyfriend Day, and on November 19, International Men’s Day.

What do blue flowers mean?

Loyalty, trust and calm. Since they are rare in nature, they also convey that the other person is one of a kind.
