Download the flower calendar

Choose the year and click the cover to download the PDF. Both have the same dates, but the movable dates, such as Mother’s Day or Amor y Amistad, are worked out for each year.

Prefer your phone to remind you? There is also the calendar in .ics format, which alerts you three days before each date, and the Google Calendar subscription.

What’s included

  1. A cover with the wheel of the year. The twelve months in a circle, each with its own color, and a flower on each date of the year when flowers are given. It is the same wheel as on the website’s home page, designed to hang on the wall.
  2. The 26 dates, month by month. For each month, the day, what is celebrated, which flower is given and where: from Valentine’s Day, Sant Jordi or each country’s Mother’s Day to the yellow, blue and purple flower trends.
  3. Seasonal flowers. A table of the flowers at their best each month in Spain, in Mexico and Central America and in Argentina, Chile and Uruguay, plus a quick guide to the meaning of each color.

How to print it

  • Size: portrait A4. In the Americas many printers use US Letter paper; if that’s your case, tick “Fit to page” so nothing gets cut off.
  • Scale: 100% or “Actual size” if you use A4. The margins are already included in the PDF.
  • Color or black and white: both work. In black and white the flowers are told apart by their outlines and the text reads just as well.
  • Paper: to hang it on the fridge or the wall, 120 to 160 gsm paper holds up better than standard 80 gsm.
  • Double-sided: to save paper, print pages 2 and 3 double-sided and keep the cover separate to hang up.

Ideas for using it

The calendar is designed so you never miss a date, but you can get more out of it:

  • At home: on the fridge or next to your desk, circling the dates that matter to you.
  • In your planner: the dates page fits in a folder or at the back of a planner, to check before each month.
  • As a gift: printed on good paper, with the first flower of the year already bought.

Do you run a flower shop, or are you a teacher who wants to use it with customers or in class? Write to us and we’ll talk.

Using the calendar

The calendar is free for personal use: downloading it, printing it and keeping it at home. For any other use, such as printing it for a business, handing it out, publishing it on another website or using it in class, write to us first from the contact page. If you want to recommend it, share the link to this page: it will always have the latest version. If you are a journalist, there is more information on the press page.

If you see a mistake or a date from your country is missing, write to us: we’ll look into it and, if it meets the criteria, add it to the next version of the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is the printable flower calendar free?

Yes. You can download and print it for free, without signing up, for personal use. If you want someone else to have it, share the link to this page with them.

Can it be printed in black and white?

Yes. All the text is black on white and the flowers have outlines, so it reads well on a printer without color. In color it looks brighter, because each month has its own.

What size is the calendar?

It is a three-page PDF in portrait A4: cover, dates for the twelve months and seasonal flowers. If your printer uses US Letter paper, choose “Fit to page” when printing.

Can I use the calendar in my flower shop or in class?

The calendar is for personal use. If you want to print it for your business, hand it out or use it in class, write to us from the contact page and we’ll talk: usually crediting Florario is enough.

Are the movable dates worked out?

Yes. Mother’s Day, Father’s Day and Amor y Amistad change day every year, and each PDF already has them worked out for its year.
